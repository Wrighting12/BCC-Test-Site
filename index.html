About this assistant

The BCC Documentation Assistant provides general guidance on UK Certificates of Origin and Chamber certification procedures. It is advisory only and does not issue certificates or make binding determinations.

Important

Do not enter personal data or upload documents into this chat. Responsibility for origin declarations and acceptance of goods remains with the exporter and issuing Chamber.
